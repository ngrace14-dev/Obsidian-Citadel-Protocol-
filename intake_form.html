Agency Services & Pricing

Standardized billing framework for development, logic, and care.

Our pricing model is engineered to provide absolute transparency. Visual and UI structural work is billed at fixed, predictable rates, while custom logic and advanced integrations are scoped according to dedicated engineering time.

1. New Files & Views (Base Development)

$250 per New File / View

Applied to the creation of standard, static web architecture. Each core page, component view, or functional route added to the project is billed as a single flat-rate unit.

  • New page or component view
  • Responsive design implementation
  • Standard contact forms & basic webhooks
  • Testing & deployment

2. Advanced Engineering Engagement

$1,000 Upfront Retainer + $125 / hour

Complex systems requiring data architecture, custom logic, or third-party API configurations fall under the Engineering Engagement model. The $1,000 retainer covers initial scoping, database modeling, and technical architecture prior to development.

Engineering Feature Triggers:

✓ Custom CRM Integrations
✓ User Authentication & Roles
✓ Database Architecture
✓ Artificial Intelligence (AI)
✓ 3rd-Party API Connections
✓ Complex Automations
✓ Custom SaaS Functionality
✓ Dynamic User Dashboards

3. Maintenance & Retainer Plans

Essential Care $99/mo

  • Security review & updates
  • Uptime monitoring & backups
  • Up to 30 mins structural changes/mo
  • $125 Change Minimum Fee Waived

Growth Care $249/mo

  • Everything in Essential Care
  • Priority task scheduling
  • Up to 2 hrs structural changes/mo
  • $125 Change Minimum Fee Waived

4. Priority & Expedited Operations

1.5x Standard Rates

Projects requiring aggressive turnarounds, "skip-the-line" scheduling, or execution outside of standard business hours are subject to priority pricing.

  • Expedited New File: $375 per file/view
  • Expedited Engineering: $187.50 per hour

5. Infrastructure & Managed Services

All domains, hosting environments, third-party services, and software subscriptions remain the legal property of the client whenever possible. Our agency operates through authorized administrative access to ensure long-term client independence and portability.

Available Managed Services

We can source, configure, manage, and maintain:

  • Domain Registration & DNS Management
  • Web Hosting & Server Infrastructure
  • SSL Certificates
  • Business Email Configuration
  • SaaS Platform Administration
  • Third-Party Integrations
  • API Subscription Management
  • Security Monitoring & Maintenance
  • Website Backups & Recovery

Ownership & Billing

Clients may choose either:

  • Client-Owned Infrastructure
    • Accounts remain in the client's name
    • Services are billed directly by the provider
    • Agency receives delegated access
  • Agency-Managed Infrastructure
    • Infrastructure is provisioned and managed by our agency
    • Hosting, maintenance, monitoring, and related services are billed under the selected service plan
    • Administrative ownership may be transferred to the client upon request, subject to the terms of the service agreement

Important Note

Third-party costs such as hosting fees, domain renewals, email services, SaaS subscriptions, premium plugins, and API usage charges are separate from development fees unless explicitly included in a managed service package.
